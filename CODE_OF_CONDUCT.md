# Code of conduct

## Our standard

NSDeck contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and productive. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes; and
- protecting credentials, infrastructure details, and other sensitive data shared by mistake.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, its issue and review discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project or Quantex Secure.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. When appropriate, use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting channel so sensitive details are not exposed publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of people who report concerns.
